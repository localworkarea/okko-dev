Frequently Asked Questions

How long will it take?
We’ll estimate the time and cost after inspecting the vehicle and before starting the work. If the vehicle needs to stay for a few days, we’ll tell you in advance.

Do you repair the control unit or replace it right away?
In most cases, the control unit can be repaired — it’s cheaper than replacing it, and there’s no need to wait for a new part.

How long does programming take?
It depends on the control unit. We’ll give you an estimated time after diagnostics, before starting the work.

Do you work with all brands?
Yes. We have the most experience with Mercedes-Benz Actros, including keys and control units.
