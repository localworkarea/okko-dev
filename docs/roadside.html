Broken down on the road?

We’ll come to you with the necessary equipment and carry out the repair on-site. It’s faster and cheaper than towing the truck to the workshop.

How we help

Two options
  • Main option

    Repair directly on the road

    A mobile technician arrives with tools and diagnostic equipment and carries out the repair on-site. The truck can continue its journey.

    Most issues with the electrical system, pneumatics, engine, or minor mechanical faults can be fixed without towing.

  • If on-site repair isn’t possible

    Towing arrangements

    If the repair cannot be completed on-site, we’ll arrange for the truck to be towed to our workshop in Perl.

    While the truck is being repaired, the driver can wait comfortably in our rest area.

Before we arrive

  1. 1

    Set up a warning triangle and turn on your hazard lights.

  2. 2

    Have the following details ready: make, model, license plate number.

  3. 3

    Tell us your exact location — motorway, direction of travel, kilometer marker, or nearest exit.

  4. 4

    Describe what happened: which warning lights are on, any unusual sounds, and when the problem started.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will it take you to arrive?
It depends on where you are and what’s wrong with the vehicle. We’ll give you an estimated arrival time over the phone.

How much does the call-out cost?
We’ll tell you the cost over the phone before we come out. No unexpected charges on-site.

Do you work at night and on weekends?
Yes. Our phone line is available 24/7, seven days a week.

What if a spare part is needed?
Our technician carries the most commonly needed parts. If a specific part is required, we’ll let you know how quickly we can get it.
